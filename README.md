## Understanding Cashback Confirmation Timelines

Your joining bonus only deposits once cashback confirms, not when it's pending. This timeline confusion trips up most new members.

**Immediate (Hours):** Your purchase appears as pending cashback the same day, sometimes within minutes. This confirms TopCashback tracked it successfully. Don't celebrate yet—pending isn't payable.

**30 Days:** Most retail purchases (Amazon, John Lewis, high-street brands) flip from pending to confirmed within 30 days. The retailer's system confirms the purchase wasn't returned or cancelled.

**60-90 Days:** Travel bookings, insurance quotes, and broadband switches typically take the full window. The retailer needs time to confirm the policy was issued, the booking was completed, and the delivery/service fulfilled.

**6+ Months:** Contract products (mobile, energy) occasionally take this long. The retailer's back-end takes time to reconcile commissions through their entire confirmation process.

**Never Confirms:** A small percentage of purchases (typically 2-5%) decline even from pending. TopCashback flags these as non-confirmable after the merchant's investigation. You'll never see the bonus on these transactions.

The key: cashback sitting in pending for 90+ days is normal. Stop checking obsessively. Once it hits 120 days without confirmation and shows no progress, contact support.
